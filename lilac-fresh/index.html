Frequently asked questions

How long does a cleaning take?

Cleaning time depends on your home's size, its condition, and the service you choose. Deep and move-out cleans usually take longer than regular cleaning.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

You can stay home or arrange access in advance. Please include any access instructions when booking.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

Cleaning supplies are included. If you have preferred products or sensitive surfaces, please mention them when booking.

Can I book a weekend cleaning?

Yes, you can choose a weekend date in the booking form. Available time slots depend on the selected service and date.
